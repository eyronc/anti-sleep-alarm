# DESIGN.md: Anti-Sleep Alarm Presentation Deck

## Identity & Voice
- **Subject**: Anti-Sleep Alarm (Autonomous IoT Slumber Deterrent for Students).
- **Tone**: Technical, witty, pragmatic, authentic student-engineering reality.
- **Dials**: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Typography
- **Headlines & Body**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif.
  - Weight 800/700 for titles (geometric grotesk, double-storey 'a', tracking -0.035em).
  - Weight 500/600/700 for body copy, cards, and metadata.
- **Handwritten Script Accent**: `Caveat` / `Satisfy`, cursive (used strictly for the "Group 1" cover overlay).
- **Code & Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace.

## Palette
- **Canvas Base**: `#efeee9` / `#ededed` (Canva off-white minimalist canvas).
- **Dark Mode**: `#101114` (Obsidian matte).
- **Ink Primary**: `#111215` (deep charcoal black, high contrast WCAG AAA).
- **Ink Secondary**: `#454752` (readable slate).
- **Accent Red**: `#d92525` (physical escalation & buzzer alert).
- **Accent Emerald**: `#059669` (normal telemetry state).
- **Accent Amber**: `#d97706` (warning state).

## Layout & Rules
- **Viewport**: Fixed 100vw x 100vh with `overflow: hidden`. Every slide must fit on screen without vertical scrolling.
- **No Pop-Ups**: Zero floating bars or pop-up pills obscuring slide content.
- **Navigation**: Keyboard driven (`ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Space`, `F` for fullscreen, `P` for PPTX export, `T` for theme) plus a slim unobtrusive bottom progress line and discrete corner indicator.
- **Anti-Slop Standards**:
  - No em dashes (`—`) anywhere.
  - Real formulas and real working hardware/software models.
  - Full keyboard accessibility.
